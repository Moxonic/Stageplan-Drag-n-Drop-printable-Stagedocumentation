Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

StagePlanner is a tool for drawing stage plans and technical documentation for shows. This policy explains what personal data StagePlanner handles, why, and what rights you have. It applies to the app at stageplanner.netlify.app.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Daniel Soto Delgado, a private individual in Norway, who runs StagePlanner.

Contact for anything about privacy: pinknoiceharmony@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • To use StagePlanner you sign in with Google, or with a code sent to your email address where that option is offered. That account is the main personal data we keep.
  • Your stage plans, shows, scenes and saved stages are stored in your own browser. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • If usage statistics are switched on, and only if you agree to them, we count which features are used. We never collect the contents of your plans.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. What stays in your browser

StagePlanner saves your work in your browser's local storage on your own device: the current show, its scenes and shifts, your saved stages and storage lists, your colour palettes, your light or dark theme, and your sign-in session. This is needed for the app to work. It never leaves your device unless you save a file yourself or export a PDF.

Because it lives in your browser, clearing your browser data deletes it, and we cannot recover it for you. Save show files if you want a backup.

StagePlanner does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Who we share data with

We use the following service providers (data processors) to run StagePlanner. They process data only on our instructions.

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) handles accounts, sign-in and sending sign-in codes by email.
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc.) hosts the website.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) handles sign-in, if you choose Continue with Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) provides usage statistics, only when they are switched on and you have agreed to them.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) delivers two open-source libraries the app uses to create PDFs. Like any web server, it receives your IP address when your browser loads them.

We may also disclose data if Norwegian law requires it.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers are based in the United States or may process data there. When that happens, the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you ask us to delete your account. We also delete accounts that have not been used for 3 years.
  • Sign-in security data and server logs: kept for a limited time by our providers, normally no more than 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to 2 years, then deleted. They are also deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Emails with us: as long as needed to handle your question.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, as it applies in Norway through the Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven), you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • have wrong data corrected
  • have your data deleted, including your whole account
  • restrict or object to how we use your data
  • get your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent to usage statistics at any time, without affecting what was done before

To use any of these rights, email pinknoiceharmony@gmail.com from the address on your account. We answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or to the authority in the country where you live.

9. Children

StagePlanner is not meant for children under 13, and they should not create an account.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in uses no passwords, so there are none to leak. Access to account data on the server is limited by database access rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you and Datatilsynet as the law requires if a breach puts your data at risk.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example new data being collected, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
